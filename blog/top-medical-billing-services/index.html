2

Advanced Medical Practice Management (AMPM)

AMPM is a comprehensive medical billing and practice management company that focuses on maximizing revenue for healthcare providers. They offer a full suite of services including coding, claims submission, and denial management.

Specialties Served: Primary Care, Internal Medicine, Cardiology, Dermatology, Orthopedics, Gastroenterology, Urology, Mental Health, and many more.

Pricing Model: Primarily percentage-based (estimated 5-8% of collections), with potential for per-claim or flat fee for specific services.

  • ✅ Extensive experience with a wide range of specialties, indicating adaptability.
  • ✅ Focus on proactive denial management and appeal processes to improve collections.
  • ⚠️ Pricing may be higher for smaller practices due to their comprehensive service model.
3

Medimax Medical Billing

Medimax Medical Billing provides tailored medical billing solutions designed to reduce administrative burden and improve financial performance for healthcare practices. They emphasize transparency and client communication.

Specialties Served: Family Practice, Pediatrics, OB/GYN, Chiropractic, Physical Therapy, Urgent Care, Behavioral Health.

Pricing Model: Percentage-based (estimated 4-7% of collections), with potential for per-claim options for high-volume, low-value claims.

  • ✅ Strong emphasis on client communication and personalized service.
  • ✅ Offers detailed reporting and analytics to help practices understand their financial health.
  • ⚠️ May have a more limited scope of highly specialized services compared to larger firms.
4

Medical Billing Services of Georgia (MBSG)

MBSG offers professional medical billing and coding services to healthcare providers across Georgia. They are committed to accuracy, compliance, and maximizing reimbursement for their clients.

Specialties Served: General Surgery, Anesthesiology, Radiology, Pathology, ENT, Oncology, Podiatry, Pain Management.

Pricing Model: Percentage-based (estimated 4-7% of collections) or per-claim (estimated $3-$6 per claim), depending on practice size and volume.

  • ✅ Strong focus on compliance with current billing regulations.
  • ✅ Offers flexible pricing models to accommodate different practice needs.
  • ⚠️ Some online reviews suggest occasional delays in initial setup for new clients.
5

Precision Practice Management

Precision Practice Management is a full-service medical billing and revenue cycle management company. They leverage technology and experienced staff to streamline billing processes and optimize collections.

Specialties Served: Cardiology, Orthopedics, Urology, Gastroenterology, Dermatology, Plastic Surgery, Nephrology, Allergy & Immunology.

Pricing Model: Primarily percentage-based (estimated 5-8% of collections), with potential for flat fees for specific consulting services.

  • ✅ Utilizes advanced technology for efficient claims processing and denial management.
  • ✅ Offers comprehensive revenue cycle management beyond just billing.
  • ⚠️ Their extensive technology integration might have a steeper learning curve for some practice staff.
6

Synergy Healthcare Solutions

Synergy Healthcare Solutions provides end-to-end medical billing and practice management support. They aim to improve practice efficiency and financial outcomes through customized solutions.

Specialties Served: Internal Medicine, Family Medicine, Pediatrics, Psychiatry, Urgent Care, Chiropractic, Physical Therapy, Optometry.

Pricing Model: Percentage-based (estimated 4-7% of collections) or flat fee for smaller practices or specific project work.

  • ✅ Offers tailored solutions to meet the unique needs of each practice.
  • ✅ Strong emphasis on improving overall practice efficiency, not just billing.
  • ⚠️ May not be the most cost-effective option for very high-volume, low-reimbursement specialties.
7

ProMD

ProMD offers comprehensive medical billing and practice management services with a focus on maximizing physician reimbursement and reducing administrative burdens. They emphasize a partnership approach with their clients.

Specialties Served: Anesthesiology, Radiology, Pathology, Emergency Medicine, Cardiology, Orthopedics, Gastroenterology, Urology.

Pricing Model: Percentage-based (estimated 4.5-8% of collections) or per-claim (estimated $3-$7 per claim) for certain high-volume specialties.

  • ✅ Strong expertise in complex and high-value specialties.
  • ✅ Offers detailed financial reporting and analytics to track performance.
  • ⚠️ Their focus on complex specialties might make them less ideal for very small, general practices.
